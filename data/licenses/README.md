# License bundle staging

This directory contains engineering policy metadata only. No distributable license bundle or dataset has been approved. Official primary URLs are referenced; policy response bytes were retrieved for internal research, checksummed, then discarded. `primary-fetch-evidence.json` records actual fetch metadata separately from raw dataset hashes. Do not use a policy-page digest as a data-snapshot digest.

Before a pack can leave internal engineering review, materialize its applicable license texts/notices with current source versions, visible OSM attribution, offline-accessible copyright/license information, intended-use rights evidence, and any applicable ODbL derivative database/alteration offer. Human review must approve the actual arrangement. Menu text and individual imagery need independently recorded rights, as do map style/fonts/glyphs/sprites. Parent policy ACCEPT is not publishing permission.

Primary references: [ODbL 1.0 legal code](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [OSMF attribution](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines), [public raster tile service policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/). Current policy research reviewed 2026-10-09; re-review at acquisition and export, and on any terms change.
